THE CURRICULUM

The 10-Week Teaching Series

Ten progressive lessons examining the character, consequences, responsibility, and restoration surrounding a man's words.

01
WEEK ONE · MATTHEW 12:36–37

A Man’s Mouth Reveals His Character

Examine the spiritual weight of spoken words and the accountability every man carries before God.

02
WEEK TWO · PROVERBS 17:9

Confidentiality Is a Measure of Brotherhood

WEEK THREE · PRAYER & DISCERNMENT

Gossip Disguised as Concern

Recognize when spiritual language becomes cover for spreading information that was never ours to share.

READ LESSON ↗

WEEK FOUR · JAMES 3:5–6

A Man Who Cannot Control His Tongue Cannot Be Trusted With Greater Responsibility

Consider the destructive reach of uncontrolled speech and the integrity required for leadership.

READ LESSON ↗
05
WEEK FIVE · PROVERBS 16:28

The Talebearer Does Not Merely Tell Stories. He Creates Division.

06
WEEK SIX · PROVERBS 18:8

The Spirit of Gossip Loves Information More Than Integrity

WEEK SEVEN · PSALM 15:1–3

WEEK EIGHT · EPHESIANS 4:29
