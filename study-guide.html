MEN'S MINISTRY · ONE CLASS PER WEEK · 10 WEEKS

MEN WHO CARRY SECRETS

The Weight of a Man's Words, the Integrity of His Brotherhood, and the Sin of Gossip

Weekly Class Format · 90 Minutes

Opening Scripture and Prayer10 minutes
Weekly Teaching · one lesson per class60 minutes
Men's Examination and Discussion15 minutes
Closing Prayer and Accountability Commitment5 minutes

Total: 90 minutes. Each class meets once per week for ten weeks. The examination and closing prayer are recurring components of every session.

The 10-Week Teaching Series

WEEK 1

A Man's Mouth Reveals His Character

Matthew 12:36–37

WEEK 2

Confidentiality Is a Measure of Brotherhood

Proverbs 17:9

WEEK 3

A Prayer Request Should Never Become a Spiritual Permission Slip for Gossip

WEEK 4

A Man Who Cannot Control His Tongue Cannot Be Trusted With Greater Responsibility

James 3:5–6

WEEK 5

The Talebearer Does Not Merely Tell Stories. He Creates Division.

Proverbs 16:28

WEEK 6

The Spirit of Gossip Loves Information More Than Integrity

Proverbs 18:8

WEEK 7

The Man Who Repeats Confidential Information Must Examine His Own Integrity

Psalm 15:1–3

WEEK 8

Real Men Correct Gossip Instead of Participating

Ephesians 4:29

WEEK 9

Repentance Requires More Than Saying, “I Didn't Mean To”

Matthew 5:23–24

WEEK 10

Become the Man Whose Presence Makes People Safer

Galatians 6:1–2

Recurring Class Components

Every week concludes with The Men's Examination: Weekly Personal Accountability, followed by the Closing Prayer and a personal commitment to apply the lesson.
