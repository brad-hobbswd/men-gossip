THE MEN'S EXAMINATION Weekly Personal Accountability Before leaving each class, every man should take time to examine his own heart, conduct, conversations, and relationships. These questions are not intended to measure another man's failures or provide ammunition for judgment. They are designed to bring personal accountability before God. Questions for Personal Accountability 1. Have I ever repeated something someone told me in confidence? 2. Have I ever used the phrase “I'm only telling you because I care” to justify sharing private information? 3. Have I ever requested details about someone's personal struggle without a legitimate reason? 4. Have I ever repeated a prayer request that was not mine to share? 5. Have I ever contributed to a conversation that damaged another man's reputation? 6. Have I ever laughed at gossip instead of correcting the conversation? 7. Have I ever shared information to make myself feel important, informed, connected, or superior? 8. Have I ever betrayed someone's trust and then minimized the consequences? 9. Can men safely share their struggles with me? 10. Does my private conversation reflect the same integrity I claim publicly? Personal Reflection Am I becoming a man whose words protect, whose presence strengthens, whose counsel restores, and whose integrity can be trusted? The purpose of this examination is not merely to recognize the sin of gossip in others. The purpose is to allow God to examine us, correct us, and develop within us the integrity necessary to become trustworthy brothers. I would make this a recurring section at the end of every weekly lesson, perhaps with the heading “The Men's Examination: Weekly Accountability”, so the men develop a consistent habit of self-examination rather than treating accountability as a one-time exercise.
