MEN WHO CARRY
SECRETS

The Weight of a Man's Words, the Integrity of His Brotherhood, and the Sin of Gossip

A Scripture-based challenge to guard our words, protect confidence, walk in integrity, and build brotherhood that restores rather than divides.

By Bradley Hobbs

View the Teaching GuideApostolic Foundation

Core Scriptures

“A talebearer revealeth secrets: but he that is of a faithful spirit concealeth the matter.”
Proverbs 11:13 KJV
“He that goeth about as a talebearer revealeth secrets: therefore meddle not with him that flattereth with his lips.”
Proverbs 20:19 KJV
“A whisperer separateth chief friends.”
Proverbs 16:28 KJV

The Purpose

This teaching confronts gossip, whispering, betrayal of confidence, careless speech, and the misuse of another man's vulnerability. The goal is not to conceal wrongdoing or avoid biblical accountability. The goal is to distinguish righteous correction from sinful exposure and to become men whose words, conduct, and brotherhood reflect the character of Jesus Christ.

Walk in holiness. Speak truth. Protect confidence. Correct in righteousness. Restore in meekness. Bear one another's burdens. Refuse division. Love your brother.
