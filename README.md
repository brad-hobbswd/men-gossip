# MEN WHO CARRY SECRETS

**The Weight of a Man's Words, the Integrity of His Brotherhood, and the Sin of Gossip**

A ten-week Apostolic men's discipleship and teaching series by **Bradley Hobbs**.

> A man's mouth should protect what his character claims to value.

## About the Study

*Men Who Carry Secrets* confronts gossip, whispering, betrayal of confidence, careless speech, division, and the misuse of another man's vulnerability. The study calls men to examine their conversations, protect trust, correct in righteousness, and pursue repentance and restoration according to Scripture.

The goal is not simply to identify gossip in others. The goal is to become men whose words can be trusted, whose presence makes people safer, and whose brotherhood strengthens rather than divides.

## Ten-Week Teaching Series

1. **A Man's Mouth Reveals His Character**
2. **Confidentiality Is a Measure of Brotherhood**
3. **A Prayer Request Should Never Become a Spiritual Permission Slip for Gossip**
4. **A Man Who Cannot Control His Tongue Cannot Be Trusted With Greater Responsibility**
5. **The Talebearer Does Not Merely Tell Stories. He Creates Division.**
6. **The Spirit of Gossip Loves Information More Than Integrity**
7. **The Man Who Repeats Confidential Information Must Examine His Own Integrity**
8. **Real Men Correct Gossip Instead of Participating**
9. **Repentance Requires More Than Saying, “I Didn't Mean To”**
10. **Become the Man Whose Presence Makes People Safer**

## Weekly Class Format

Each week is designed as one class session, approximately 90 minutes:

| Activity | Time |
|---|---:|
| Opening Scripture and prayer | 10 minutes |
| Teaching and discussion | 60 minutes |
| Personal examination and discussion | 15 minutes |
| Closing prayer and accountability commitment | 5 minutes |
| **Total** | **90 minutes** |
