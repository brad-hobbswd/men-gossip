TO BE PRAYED AT THE CONCLUSION OF EVERY CLASS

Closing Prayer

Lord Jesus, search our hearts and examine our words. Reveal every place where pride, curiosity, insecurity, jealousy, or careless conversation has influenced our mouths. Forgive us for every confidence we have violated and every conversation we have entertained that brought harm to another brother.

Teach us to become men of integrity. Give us wisdom to know when to speak, courage to correct ungodly conversation, humility to acknowledge our failures, and strength to guard information entrusted to us.

Help us become men who restore rather than destroy, protect rather than expose, encourage rather than wound, and strengthen rather than divide.

Let our brotherhood become a place of honesty, accountability, prayer, restoration, and genuine trust. May our words bring life. May our silence demonstrate wisdom. May our conduct reflect the character of Jesus Christ.

Make us men whose mouths can be trusted, whose hearts are clean, and whose brotherhood is strong.

In Jesus' name, Amen.

This prayer closes every class with personal examination, repentance, commitment to integrity, and prayer for stronger brotherhood.

MEN WHO CARRY SECRETS · Bradley Hobbs
