MEN WHO CARRY SECRETS · ABOUT THE AUTHOR

About the Author

Bradley Hobbs

Bradley Hobbs is a Christian writer, teacher, and ministry contributor whose work centers on biblical truth, spiritual growth, discipleship, and the practical responsibilities of Christian living. His writing challenges readers to examine their faith beyond public expression and to allow Scripture to shape their character, relationships, and everyday conduct.

Through teaching resources and devotional writing, Bradley seeks to encourage believers to pursue a deeper walk with Jesus Christ, strengthen their understanding of Scripture, and live out their faith with integrity, compassion, and conviction.

Men Who Carry Secrets reflects that commitment through a direct examination of gossip, confidentiality, spiritual maturity, brotherhood, repentance, and restoration.

Author's Purpose

This teaching is intended to encourage men to become trustworthy brothers whose words protect, whose presence strengthens, and whose conduct reflects the character of Christ. The challenge is personal: allow the Word of God to examine us before we use the Word to examine someone else.
