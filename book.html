MEN WHO CARRY SECRETS · ABOUT THE TEACHING

About the Teaching

The Weight of a Man's Words, the Integrity of His Brotherhood, and the Sin of Gossip

By Bradley Hobbs

Men Who Carry Secrets is a ten-week men's discipleship teaching confronting gossip, whispering, betrayal of confidence, careless speech, and the misuse of another man's vulnerability.

The study calls men to examine the difference between biblical accountability and sinful exposure, righteous correction and personal humiliation, spiritual concern and curiosity, and restoration and division.

Study Structure

One class per week over ten weeks. Each class follows a 90-minute format: opening Scripture and prayer, weekly teaching, personal examination and discussion, and a closing prayer with an accountability commitment.

View the 10-Week GuideRead the Apostolic Foundation

Central Message

Walk in holiness. Speak truth. Protect confidence. Correct in righteousness. Restore in meekness. Bear one another's burdens. Refuse division. Love your brother.
